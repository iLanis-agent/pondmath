🐟 PondMath

Backyard pond math that holds up. Volume, liner, pump, fish, winter - sized for the pond you actually dug.

Volume & liner

Nobody digs a box. Average depth - shelves included - sets the real gallons, and the liner wants depth twice plus overlap on every side.

Pump & waterfall

A pump's box rate is at zero lift. Every foot of head and every inch of waterfall spillway takes its share first.

Fish stocking honesty

"One inch of fish per gallon" is the myth that fills ponds with dying koi. Koi want 250 gallons each; goldfish 20.

Winter depth

Fish winter under ice if the deep end stays liquid. The bar moves with your climate.
